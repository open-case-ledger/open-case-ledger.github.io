Research note · updated August 7, 2026

Pamela Leigh Walton

Working address and court-record timeline. These notes separate documented facts from unresolved questions.

01

Spring 1988 · 520 Chestnut Street

An April 25, 1988 newspaper brief identified Pamela L. Walton, age 24, with an address of 520 Chestnut Street in Harrodsburg, Kentucky. Open Case Ledger has asked the Mercer District Court clerk to locate the underlying April 1988 court record and provide the case number, incident date, complete charges, dispositions, archive locator, and remote-copy instructions.

02

Property record

Mercer County deed material shows Danny Lawson and Mary Jo Lawson acquired the Chestnut Street property on February 27, 1986 from Lonnie and Phyllis Campbell for $19,000. The deed lists the Lawsons at 219 Bonta Lane. Property ownership does not establish who occupied 520 Chestnut in 1988, so directory and local-history research remains open.

03

June 27, 1988 · 319 Walnut

A full July 6, 1988 Lexington Herald-Leader court-results page supplied by Lexington Public Library records a June 27 Fayette County District Court entry for Pamela Walton, age 25, of 319 Walnut in Carlisle. The published disposition was loitering, a $250 fine, amended from a loitering-for-the-purpose-of-prostitution allegation. We use this only as a dated address and court-contact anchor.

04

Open questions

We are tracing who occupied or was associated with 520 Chestnut and 319 Walnut, when Pamela began using each address, and whether surviving police or court records clarify her movements and associates in the months before her death. Lexington police/index records and Mercer County local-history research remain pending.

Source note: the Herald-Leader page is summarized here rather than republished in full. The complete newspaper scan is preserved in the project archive. An alternate name used in legacy research, “Lee Allen Walton,” remains unverified and is not treated here as established biography.
