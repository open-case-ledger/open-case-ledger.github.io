Research note · updated August 11, 2026

Pamela Leigh Walton

Current research updates are listed newest first, followed by older documented findings.

09

August 11, 2026 · CourtNet search did not resolve the alternate-name lead

A public Kentucky CourtNet search returned no result for Pamela Walton. A search for Lee Walton returned six 2026 guardianship matters that are outside the 1980–1988 target period and appear unrelated by date and case type. This is recorded as an online-index negative, not proof that no historical name-change case exists. Kentucky Court of Justice guidance says older records may be stored outside the current clerk system, and Fayette County states that records 36 years and older are stored by the Kentucky Department for Libraries and Archives. The historical Fayette Probate index therefore remains the next locator step.

08

August 10, 2026 · Historical name-change retrieval route identified

Kentucky Department for Libraries and Archives staff explained that KDLA cannot locate a 1980–1988 name-change case by name alone. The Probate Division of the District Court in the filing county must first locate the case and supply the case number, accession number, storage location, and box number. Fayette and Mercer counties are the current search targets. The alternate name “Lee Allen Walton” remains an unverified research lead unless a primary court record establishes it.

07

August 10, 2026 · 1988 telephone-directory results received

David Kirkpatrick of the Mercer County Public Library History Research Center reported that Pamela was not listed in the 1984–1989 phonebooks and supplied the complete 1988 Harrodsburg white-pages section. Direct review of the 1988 scan found no Walton surname entry. Danny and Mary Jo Lawson, who owned the 520 Chestnut property during the relevant period, were each listed at 219 Bonta Lane. These results show that neither Pamela nor the titled owners were publicly listed at 520 Chestnut in that directory; they do not prove the property was vacant or identify an unlisted occupant.

06

August 10, 2026 · 319 Walnut property records received

Nicholas County property records supplied by Jackie M. Earlywine show that Estill H. Walton and Leona Walton acquired 319 Walnut in 1954. The current property card references a January 13, 1999 deed from Estill and Leona, and a March 6, 2024 deed completes the later Walton ownership chain. Pamela is not named in any of the three records. The documents establish a Walton ownership history connected to the address, not Pamela’s ownership or occupancy.

05

Current status · August 10, 2026

The ownership question for 319 Walnut has narrowed, but the important question remains open: who actually occupied or received mail there in June 1988, and how was Pamela connected to the Walton owners? Lexington police/index records, the Mercer District Court April 1988 record search, and local-history research remain pending. No additional Nicholas County PVA request is needed.

04

August 7, 2026 · Local-history search acknowledged

David Kirkpatrick of the Mercer County Public Library History Research Center acknowledged our request to search 1987–1989 local-history materials for both addresses. He said the center can provide limited searches as staff time permits. The center returned its telephone-directory findings on August 10; a focused local-newspaper microfilm search remains the next library step.

03

June 27, 1988 · 319 Walnut

A full July 6, 1988 Lexington Herald-Leader court-results page supplied by Lexington Public Library records a June 27 Fayette County District Court entry for Pamela Walton, age 25, of 319 Walnut in Carlisle. The published disposition was loitering, a $250 fine, amended from a loitering-for-the-purpose-of-prostitution allegation. We use this only as a dated address and court-contact anchor.

02

Spring 1988 · 520 Chestnut Street

An April 25, 1988 newspaper brief identified Pamela L. Walton, age 24, with an address of 520 Chestnut Street in Harrodsburg, Kentucky. Open Case Ledger has asked the Mercer District Court clerk to locate the underlying April 1988 court record and provide the case number, incident date, complete charges, dispositions, archive locator, and remote-copy instructions.

01

February 27, 1986 · Property record

Mercer County deed material shows Danny Lawson and Mary Jo Lawson acquired the Chestnut Street property from Lonnie and Phyllis Campbell for $19,000. The deed lists the Lawsons at 219 Bonta Lane. Property ownership does not establish who occupied 520 Chestnut in 1988, so directory and local-history research remains open.

Source note: the Herald-Leader page and the Nicholas County property records are summarized here rather than republished in full. The complete newspaper scan, property card, deed records, and 1988 telephone-directory scan are preserved in the project archive. The 2024 deed contains current property details that are not necessary to publish. An alternate name used in legacy research, “Lee Allen Walton,” remains unverified and is not treated here as established biography.
