Fort Worth, Texas · February 18, 1977 · FWPD report 77-A-14

June Ward

Fort Worth Police Department's current cold-case listing says 25-year-old June Ward was found beside the curb in the 1000 block of West Fuller on February 18, 1977. She had been strangled. Her white Chevrolet Laguna was found separately at 4800 South Freeway.

What the released 1977 report adds: In August 2026, Fort Worth released nine heavily redacted pages of basic information from report 77-A-14. The surviving first-response narrative records that at approximately 3:04 a.m., officers were approached by a man who said he had found the body of a white woman beside the curb. The officers went to the location and found a nude female lying face-up next to the north curb of the 1000 block of West Fuller. A separate visible vehicle supplement records a white 1973 Chevrolet two-door Laguna located at approximately 2:55 a.m. The city's current cold-case page places that vehicle at 4800 South Freeway.

Historical case status: One surviving status field in the released packet is marked pending in March 1977. More importantly for the present day, Fort Worth's 2026 records response treats the responsive investigative material as information whose release would interfere with an open or pending criminal matter.

Later DNA history: A December 2025 Fort Worth Star-Telegram feature quotes June’s son Dave Ward saying that around 2011 a cold-case detective told him investigators had obtained a partial DNA sample and were sending it for testing. Ward said no match was found. He also said he has not received a clear recent answer about whether enough DNA remains for newer advanced testing. Fort Worth Police has separately partnered with Othram to digitize its cold-case files and evidence information as part of a broader technology effort. Public sources do not establish that June’s historical partial sample still survives, what biological material produced it, or whether it is suitable for forensic genetic genealogy.

Records status: Fort Worth provided the basic information at no charge and closed Open Case Ledger request E006801-080626. The city withheld the remainder of the responsive report under Texas Government Code § 552.108(a)(1), relying on prior Attorney General ruling OR2025-010303. The release therefore does not disclose the full witness accounts, investigative narrative, evidence analysis, suspect-development history, or later forensic work.

Current research path: preserve the newly documented early-morning chronology, compare it against contemporaneous newspaper coverage and later public statements, and continue tracing the origin and present status of the reported partial DNA sample.
