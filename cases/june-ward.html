June Ward

Fort Worth Police Department's current cold-case listing says 25-year-old June Ward was found beside the curb in the 1000 block of West Fuller on February 18, 1977. She had been strangled. Her white Chevrolet Laguna was found separately at 4800 South Freeway.

What the released 1977 report adds: In August 2026, Fort Worth released nine heavily redacted pages of basic information from report 77-A-14. The surviving first-response narrative records that at approximately 3:04 a.m., officers were approached by a man who said he had found the body of a white woman beside the curb. The officers went to the location and found a nude female lying face-up next to the north curb of the 1000 block of West Fuller. A separate visible vehicle supplement records a white 1973 Chevrolet two-door Laguna located at approximately 2:55 a.m. The city's current cold-case page places that vehicle at 4800 South Freeway.
