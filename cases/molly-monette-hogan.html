IdentifiedIdentity resolved · homicide investigation was ongoing in OSBI's 2024 announcement

Paoli / Garvin County, Oklahoma · Recovered June 24, 1987

Molly Monette Hogan

The woman long listed by the Oklahoma State Bureau of Investigation as the unidentified victim found in Short Creek southwest of the Interstate 35 Paoli exit was publicly identified in 2024 as 19-year-old Molly Monette Hogan.

OSBI announced on October 25, 2024 that criminalists used Investigative Genetic Genealogy to assist the identification process, after which the Office of the Chief Medical Examiner positively identified the remains as Hogan. That announcement said her cause and manner of death were being determined and that the investigation was ongoing at that time.

On August 11, 2026, the OSBI Cold Case Unit directly confirmed to Open Case Ledger that the victim had been identified and explained that the older unidentified-victim listing remained on the OSBI website because it had not yet been removed. After Open Case Ledger asked for the public identification notice, OSBI supplied the 2024 announcement.

Source-status note: the older OSBI cold-case webpage is therefore stale on the question of identity and should not be used as evidence that Hogan remains unidentified. The 2026 email confirms identification but does not itself provide a new 2026 update on the present status of the homicide investigation.

OSBI's 2024 appeal asked anyone who knew Hogan or has information about the case to contact the agency at tips@osbi.ok.gov or 1-800-522-8017.
