Active caseTPD confirms homicide remains open · records withheld

Tampa, Florida · June 24, 1995

Anthony Reed

The City of Tampa's current unsolved-homicide page says Anthony Reed was traveling eastbound near the 1400 block of East Hillsborough Avenue on June 24, 1995 when he was wounded. He later died from his injuries at St. Joseph's Hospital. Tampa Police case number: 1995-43243.

Open Case Ledger requested the original incident report. The City Clerk routed that request to the Tampa Police Department records team. On August 11, 2026, TPD Records directly confirmed that the case is still open and withheld the investigative record under Florida's active criminal intelligence and investigative information exemption.

Current research path: the investigative-record lane is closed while TPD treats the case as active. Public-source research is now focused on contemporaneous information that could create recognition cues, including the exact time of the shooting, Anthony's mode of travel or vehicle, companions or witnesses, origin and destination, and any public suspect description or appeal details.
