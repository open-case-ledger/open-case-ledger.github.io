Active caseEvidence submissions continuing · no usable DNA produced yet

Seneca / Sullivan, Missouri · Missing September 15, 2006 · Remains recovered March 15, 2007

Walter A. Mosier

The FBI’s ViCAP alert says Walter A. Mosier disappeared from Seneca, Missouri, on September 15, 2006. His skeletal remains were recovered near Interstate 44 and the Flying J Travel Center in Sullivan on March 15, 2007. The medical examiner attributed his death to homicidal violence, including blunt craniocerebral trauma and blunt and sharp trauma of the neck and chest. Franklin County identifies the investigation as case 2007-3332.

Current forensic status: Open Case Ledger asked whether testing of any evidence had produced a usable non-victim DNA profile suitable for forensic comparison. On August 14, 2026, Captain Charles W. Subke of the Franklin County Sheriff’s Office replied that no usable DNA had been produced yet and that he was still submitting evidence.

What the response establishes: as of August 14, the case had not produced usable DNA. It does not establish that all evidence has been exhausted, that no suitable evidence remains, or that future testing is impossible. Captain Subke’s statement confirms that evidence submission and forensic work are continuing.

Current research path: this exact forensic-status question is answered. Open Case Ledger is preserving the response, publishing its narrow meaning, and pausing additional outreach rather than immediately stacking another question onto an active investigator.

Source: Captain Charles W. Subke, Franklin County Sheriff’s Office, direct email correspondence to Open Case Ledger, August 14, 2026. Public case baseline: FBI ViCAP alert 2026-07-10, released July 24, 2026.
