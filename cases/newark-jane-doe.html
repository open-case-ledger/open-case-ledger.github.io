Newark, California · Found October 24, 1985

Newark Jane Doe

The FBI’s ViCAP materials list this woman as unidentified under Newark Police case 85-07156 and NamUs UP53306. Her nearly skeletal remains were found on October 24, 1985 in a field beside Mowry Avenue in Newark, approximately two miles from Interstate 880. Investigators believe she had been shot and may have died two months to one year before discovery.

The FBI estimates that she was 25–36 years old and 5 feet 6 inches to 5 feet 8 inches tall, with brown, red, or auburn shoulder-length hair. Her lower two front teeth had been missing for a substantial period before death, and the adjacent teeth had shifted together. Recovered items included red-orange dangling glass earrings, a Timex watch with a black strap, two distinctive white-metal rings, and two yellow plastic barrettes.

DNA Doe Project currently lists Mowry Wetlands Jane Doe as an active case. Its case page, last updated March 23, 2026, identifies Newark Police Detective Andrew Musantry as the agency contact. Newark Police separately directed Open Case Ledger’s records requests to Police Evidence & Records Supervisor Jeremy Beck.

Open Case Ledger retired its earlier plan to ask Newark Police broad questions about whether the agency’s public case information remains current. On August 11, 2026, OCL instead requested one primary record from the department: the releasable portion of the original incident/recovery report for case 85-07156. That report may preserve more precise recovery-location, property, discovery, or scene information that can be compared with the existing public record.

Current research focus: await and review the original incident/recovery report, continue independent public-source collection, and ask investigators a new case-specific question only if a material public-help gap remains after those sources are exhausted.
