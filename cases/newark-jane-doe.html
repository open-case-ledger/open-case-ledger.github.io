Newark, California · Found October 24, 1985

Newark Jane Doe

The FBI’s current ViCAP materials list this woman as unidentified under Newark Police case 85-07156 and NamUs UP53306. Her nearly skeletal remains were found on October 24, 1985 in a field beside Mowry Avenue in Newark, approximately two miles from Interstate 880. Investigators believe she had been shot and may have died two months to one year before discovery.

The FBI estimates that she was 25–36 years old and 5 feet 6 inches to 5 feet 8 inches tall, with brown, red, or auburn shoulder-length hair. Her lower two front teeth had been missing for a substantial period before death, and the adjacent teeth had shifted together. Recovered items included red-orange dangling glass earrings, a Timex watch with a black strap, two distinctive white-metal rings, and two yellow plastic barrettes.

Open Case Ledger contacted Newark Police on August 7, 2026 seeking current public identification-status information and any surviving identification materials that could support renewed comparison work. Detective Andrew Musantry acknowledged the inquiry on August 10 and directed the records portion of the request to the department’s Evidence & Records Supervisor. The acknowledgment did not provide new forensic-testing or identification-status findings.

Current public gap: the available official material does not yet establish the current DNA/CODIS or forensic-genetic-genealogy status, the condition or availability of retained biological and dental material, whether photographs of the recovered jewelry can now be released, or whether a newer reconstruction or public bulletin exists.
