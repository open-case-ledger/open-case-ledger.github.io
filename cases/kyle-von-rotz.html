Active caseSnohomish County records review · installment estimated by January 20, 2027

Snohomish County, Washington · June 29, 2001

Kyle Von Rotz

Kyle Von Rotz, 22, was killed on June 29, 2001 in Snohomish County. Contemporaneous reporting states that he was shot multiple times and found beside his 1986 Chrysler in the 22200 block of 119th Avenue SE. That reporting also described a Taurus revolver recovered at the scene as a public investigative lead. Open Case Ledger is treating those scene details as a secondary-source baseline until primary sheriff records are released.

The Snohomish County Sheriff’s Office maintains a current cold-case program for unsolved homicides and missing-person cases. Open Case Ledger requested the original incident report concerning Kyle Von Rotz. Snohomish County assigned the request tracking number K237860-081026. On August 14, 2026, the Sheriff’s Office Public Disclosure Unit formally acknowledged the PRA request and said it is coordinating the collection, exemption review, and production of responsive records. The County estimates that an installment of records, if any, will be available on or before January 20, 2027. It said it will contact the requester sooner if records become available earlier, or provide a revised estimate if more time is needed.

What we are waiting for: the primary incident report, which may clarify the original scene narrative, evidence recovered, the documented role and chain of custody of the Taurus revolver, and which portions of the investigation remain releasable more than two decades later. No records, fee estimate, denial, or clarification request accompanied the August 14 acknowledgment.
