Snohomish County, Washington · June 29, 2001

Kyle Von Rotz

Kyle Von Rotz, 22, was killed on June 29, 2001 in Snohomish County. Contemporaneous reporting states that he was shot multiple times and found beside his 1986 Chrysler in the 22200 block of 119th Avenue SE. That reporting also described a Taurus revolver recovered at the scene as a public investigative lead. Open Case Ledger is treating those scene details as a secondary-source baseline until primary sheriff records are released.

The Snohomish County Sheriff’s Office maintains a current cold-case program for unsolved homicides and missing-person cases. Open Case Ledger submitted a request for the original incident report concerning Kyle Von Rotz. On August 10, 2026, Snohomish County acknowledged the request as K237860-081026 and said it was being assessed to determine the applicable records-law process and the county department that should handle it. The county said the assigned department should contact the requester within five business days.

What we are waiting for: the primary incident report, which may clarify the original scene narrative, evidence recovered, the documented role and chain of custody of the Taurus revolver, and which portions of the investigation remain releasable more than two decades later.
