Refugio County, Texas · Found March 2, 1992

Refugio County Jane Doe

Texas Department of Public Safety says this unidentified woman was found March 2, 1992 on a ranch along FM 2678 in Refugio County. DPS’s June 10, 2026 cold-case release states that she remains unidentified, died from a gunshot wound, and that investigators continue exploring forensic testing. The release also says a 2020 anthropological analysis supported an updated facial reconstruction.

Open Case Ledger submitted a public-information request seeking current public identification materials and identifiers, the finalized anthropological report, completed DNA/CODIS/SNP/forensic-genetic-genealogy reports, a retained-evidence inventory, and any finalized public testing-status summary. Texas DPS formally acknowledged that request on August 10, 2026 as P021830-081026 and says it is being processed within state law.

What is still unresolved: the acknowledgment does not establish what modern identification testing has actually been completed, whether suitable biological material remains available, or whether forensic investigative genetic genealogy has been attempted. Those questions remain pending the substantive records response.
