Alachua County Baby Jane Doe

The Alachua County Sheriff’s Office says Baby Jane Doe was an infant girl found floating in a private pond near NW 102nd Place on August 20, 2003. The Sheriff’s Office currently lists case 03-9523 as a cold case and says DNA work established that she was Black and that both parents were Black and most likely of Caribbean descent.

Current status: On August 18, 2026, Detective Sergeant Michael B. Cooper of the Alachua County Sheriff’s Office told Open Case Ledger that the matter remains an active investigation. He said he could not provide many details, but added that “the path to the end has narrowed tremendously recently.”

What that does and does not establish: the response is a meaningful current-status update and indicates recent investigative progress. It does not identify the specific forensic, genealogical, or investigative development responsible for that progress, and it does not establish that an identification is imminent.

Current research path: Open Case Ledger is preserving the direct agency response and monitoring official ACSO and NamUs sources for a formal identification or additional public update rather than pressing investigators for confidential details during an active case.
