Baldock Rest Area / Interstate 5, Clackamas County, Oregon · September 7, 1985

Alfred Vernon Houle

Oregon State Police says Alfred Vernon Houle, born June 1, 1929, was found stabbed to death at the Baldock rest area on Interstate 5 in Clackamas County on September 7, 1985. His vehicle was not stolen and he did not appear to have been robbed. OSP says Houle was traveling from Seattle to visit a friend in Oregon and that the case is currently assigned to an Oregon State Police detective.

Current records status: Open Case Ledger requested the original incident or offense report for OSP case SP85-832635. In response to request PR37790, Oregon State Police Central Records stated on August 19, 2026 that the requested records relate to an active investigation and are exempt from disclosure under ORS 192.345(3). OSP closed the records request without releasing the investigative report.

What that establishes: the homicide investigation remains active. The denial does not disclose suspect information, forensic results, witness statements, or the contents of the investigative file.

Current research path: because the exact incident-report route is closed while the case remains active, Open Case Ledger is focusing on attributable public sources that may clarify Houle's travel chronology, vehicle details, discovery circumstances, witnesses, and any publicly reported lead history.
