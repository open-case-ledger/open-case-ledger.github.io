Lithia Springs, Georgia · Found May 17, 2025

Lithia Springs John Doe

The FBI says this unidentified white male was recovered from the Chattahoochee River in Lithia Springs on May 17, 2025. He was fully dressed in two pairs of pants and two jackets, including a camouflage-colored jacket. He wore Converse All Star sneakers with zip ties replacing the shoelaces, may have had long hair or substantial facial hair, had numerous tattoos, and was missing three upper front teeth.

The FBI's public ViCAP page shows two tattoo images and says additional tattoos were documented on his back, stomach or side, chest, and leg. These features, along with the unusual footwear and layered clothing, create multiple public recognition cues that may help someone recognize him.

Open Case Ledger asked the Douglas County Sheriff's Office for publicly releasable non-graphic identification materials, including fuller tattoo documentation, clothing and property details, dental information, reconstruction imagery, and current forensic-comparison status. On August 11, 2026, Records Clerk Mackenzie Harrell said the requested records were currently unavailable under O.C.G.A. § 50-18-72(a)(4) and initially directed the inquiry to the prosecuting office at the courthouse. No responsive records were released by DCSO.

Later that day, Harrell clarified that the case remains open and under investigation with the Douglas County Sheriff's Office and cautioned that the courthouse more than likely may not have information available yet. Open Case Ledger therefore is not pursuing a courthouse records request at this time.

Current public gap: the available official material does not yet show the full documented tattoo set, detailed clothing/property identifiers, the specific missing teeth, or the current fingerprint/DNA/forensic-genetic-genealogy comparison status.
