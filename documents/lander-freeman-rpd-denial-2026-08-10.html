Public recordRequest denied · ongoing investigation

Richmond Police Department · August 10, 2026

FOIA response concerning Lander S. Freeman

Requester: Open Case Ledger

Request: Original incident report relating to the June 6, 2016 disappearance of Lander S. Freeman, last seen near the 1500 block of Silver Avenue walking toward Kings Market.

Agency determination: Richmond Police Department declined to release the requested records under Va. Code § 2.2-3706.1(C), stating that criminal investigative files relating to an ongoing criminal investigation or proceeding are excluded from mandatory disclosure.

Result: No incident report was released. This records-request lane is closed, while public-source research concerning Freeman remains open.

Privacy note: the agency's original response contained the requester's legal name and mailing address. Open Case Ledger removed that personal information from the public version and identifies the requester only as Open Case Ledger.

Issued by: Melika A. Birt, CP, Office of Legal Affairs, Richmond Police Department.
