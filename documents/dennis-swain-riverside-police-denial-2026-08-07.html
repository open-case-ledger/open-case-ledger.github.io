Open Case Ledger

To Open Case Ledger:

We have received your California Public Records Act request for Police Report P394260179.

Your request for specific reports must be denied in that the information is expressly exempt from disclosure under Government Code section 7923.600(a) as information compiled as part of an investigatory record maintained by a law enforcement agency.

The letter cites Rivero v. Superior Court (1997) 54 Cal.App.4th 1048; New York Times Co. v. Superior Court (1997) 52 Cal.App.4th 97; Williams v. Superior Court (1993) 5 Cal.4th 337; and Haynie v. Superior Court (2001) 26 Cal.4th 1061.

Sincerely,

L. Sibrian
Police Records Specialist
Riverside Police Department
