York / Lebanon County, Pennsylvania · 1973

Ruth Elizabeth Brenneman

The FBI says 14-year-old Ruth was last seen being dropped off at William Penn High School on September 24, 1973. She did not return home and was reported missing; her remains were found at Fort Indiantown Gap on October 10. NCMEC reports that her brother Michael watched her walk up the school steps, that Ruth had epilepsy requiring daily medication, and that she routinely wore sunglasses outdoors because reflected light could trigger a seizure. Why keep a public ledger?

The useful result is not always a solved case. Sometimes it is a corrected public record, a surviving file, a ruled-out comparison, or a door we can stop knocking on.

Publishing negative results prevents duplicated work and makes the next researcher less likely to waste six months wrestling the same request.

How we work

Standards before spectacle

This project is built around public information, restrained claims, and respect for victims, families, witnesses, and investigators.

1

Source it

Every factual claim should lead back to an official record, court filing, archive, or clearly labeled secondary source.

2

Show the uncertainty

A resemblance is not an identification. A lead stays a lead until the responsible agency evaluates it.

3

Protect people

No private contact details, graphic images, rumor laundering, harassment, or naming living people as suspects.

4

Route tips correctly

Information belongs with the agency responsible for the case. This site does not collect anonymous tips.

About this project

Independent research, publicly accountable.

Open Case Ledger is a private, noncommercial research project. It is not affiliated with law enforcement, NamUs, NCMEC, or the FBI. Case information may change as agencies update their records. Corrections supported by reliable sources are welcomed.

For corrections or questions, email opencaseledger@gmail.com. If you have information about a listed case, contact the responsible agency directly through the official case source.
